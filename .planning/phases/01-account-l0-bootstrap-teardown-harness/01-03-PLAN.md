<objective>
Discharge the phase's blocking pricing research flag into a committed `COSTS.md`, author the manual
account-setup runbook D-01 requires, and complete the console-only account prerequisites that every
later cost control depends on.

Purpose: two of this phase's five success criteria rest on facts that Terraform cannot establish.
Cost Explorer has no enablement API. Cost allocation tags never backfill, so activating them late
leaves a permanent, unrecoverable hole in exactly the early-experimentation window where money is
most likely to leak. And the $5 budget number itself is only meaningful if the unit prices behind it
were actually verified — the roadmap flags this as blocking.

Output: a reproducible account, a pin file that makes the wrong-account mistake loud, and a cost
model whose every figure is traceable to a source and a date.
</objective>
